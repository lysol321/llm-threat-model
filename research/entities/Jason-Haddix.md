## Role in LLM Threat Modeling
Co-creator (with Arcanum Information Security) of the Arcanum PI Taxonomy — the most detailed structured classification of prompt injection attacks. The taxonomy covers:
- Attack Intents (goals and objectives)
- Attack Techniques (execution methods)
- Attack Evasions (obfuscation methods)
- Attack Inputs (attack surfaces and input vectors)

## Relevance to This Research
The Arcanum PI Taxonomy is the primary practitioner-built reference for understanding prompt injection attack surface coverage. Unlike academic taxonomies, it was built from real red-team and bug bounty experience.
