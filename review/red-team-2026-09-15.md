## Independent adversarial review

The reviewer found five additional inconsistencies after the first corrections:

1. The signature branch still ended at LOW. It now ends at provenance verified, with separate supplier/behavioral assessment.
2. Chain C still terminated at any tool-free or HITL agent. It now continues through intermediaries until propagation or unauthorized execution is independently blocked.
3. The worked infrastructure example treated message roles as authority. It now requires separate credentials and an independent broker bound to the authenticated task and authorized action.
4. Defense summaries assumed independent layers and automatic low residual risk. They now require path-specific tests, including answer/query/renderer channels.
5. ISC text attributed a specific mechanism to the OpenAI containment incident without source support and made absolute classifier claims. Mechanism attribution was removed; ISC remains controlled research at 2/5, with deployment impact assessed separately.

Follow-up review confirmed the remaining Chain C/HITL summaries and qualified ISC detection language were corrected.
